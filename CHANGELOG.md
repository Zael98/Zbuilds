# Zbuilds

Builds are refreshed from the sites every day; a new version is published whenever any of them changes.

## Game build 1.60.1.70374 — new talent trees

- The talent trees of every class changed with this beta build: builds come from the sites' new trees.
- Builds saved from the old trees are no longer offered (their points would land on other talents). Fewer builds for now; they come back as each site updates its guides.

## v0.10.0 — Two more build sites

- **RankedBoost**: its builds for every class (DPS, healing, tanking, PvP and leveling).
- **WoW Forever Talent** (wowforevertalent.com): its own level 10–20 plans, with their point order. The guides it copies from other sites are left out, so a build is not counted twice.
- Both get their own source filter; a build that another site also recommends is shown once, with "also recommended by".
- The build bar on the talent window no longer shows on the spellbook tab.

## v0.9.0 — Options, Train all and smarter reminders

### ⚙️ Options
- New **Options** tab (also `/zb options`): turn the banner and its sound on or off, choose what it shows (next talent, Legacy challenges), the trainer list in the chat, the minimap button, and how long the banner stays (4, 6, 10 or 15 s).
- **Preview** buttons for the level-up and the Legacy banners.

### 🎉 Banners
- The level-up banner now also shows the **next talent** of your build.
- A banner when you **complete a Legacy challenge**, with the perk your plan wants next.

### 📜 Trainer
- **Train all** button on the trainer window: learns everything you can in one click; Shift-click learns only what is on your action bars.
- Spells you know but **do not have on your action bars** are shown in grey as optional, with what the rest costs.
- **Next 5 levels**: how much gold the coming levels will ask for.
- **Hunter pets and warlock demons**: their abilities are no longer listed as yours; you get the upgrades your pet or demon can learn instead. Rogue poisons are listed once you have their first rank.

### Behind the scenes
- Every release, daily ones included, is now tested before it is published.
- If Wowhead fails, the trainer prices of the last good update are kept.

## v0.8.0 — Legacy, challenges and trainer

Zbuilds becomes a progression addon: besides talent builds, it now plans your Legacy points, tracks the Legacy challenges and reminds you of your trainer.

### 🏛️ Legacy planner
- New **Legacy** tab next to Talents.
- The three Legacy trees (**Professions**, **Adventure**, **Resourcefulness**) with your learned ranks, read live from the game.
- Points **available**, **spent** and **earned on the account** (out of 81, like the game's window).
- **Plans by goal** from WoW Forever Builds: Leveling alts, Professions and gold, Travel camping and upkeep, Dying and recovering, Reputation and PvP.
- Each plan is drawn over the trees with its **point order** and the **next perk** to take, respecting every unlock requirement.
- Reminder that Legacy points **cannot be refunded**.

### 🏆 Legacy challenges
- All **66 challenges**, done and pending, with progress bars (levels, professions, honor, reputations, raids…).
- **Sorted by ease**: what you are closest to comes first.
- **Hide done** to see only what is left.
- **Click** a challenge to open it in the game's Legacy Challenges window.

### 📜 Trainer reminder
- When you **level up**, an animated banner in the middle of the screen, like the game's own: the **new spells and ranks** your trainer has for you, their icons and tooltips, and the **total cost**.
- Says whether **your gold is enough** or how much you are short.
- Ranks the trainer does not sell are named apart, with where they come from (**quest**, **drop**, **vendor**).
- Prices from Wowhead, replaced by the **real prices** (reputation discount included) the first time you open your trainer.
- `/zb trainer` shows the list any time; `/zb trainertest` previews the banner.

### Other
- Everything translated into the 11 languages.
