# Zbuilds

Builds are refreshed from the sites every day; a new version is published whenever any of them changes.

## v0.8.0 — Legacy, challenges and trainer

Zbuilds becomes a progression addon: besides talent builds, it now plans your Legacy points, tracks the Legacy challenges and reminds you of your trainer.

### 🏛️ Legacy planner
- New **Legacy** tab next to Talents.
- The three Legacy trees (**Professions**, **Adventure**, **Resourcefulness**) with your learned ranks, read live from the game.
- Points **available**, **spent** and **earned on the account** (out of 81, like the game's window).
- **Plans by goal** from WoW Forever Builds: Leveling alts, Professions and gold, Travel camping and upkeep, Dying and recovering, Reputation and PvP.
- Each plan is drawn over the trees with its **point order** and the **next perk** to take, respecting every unlock requirement.
- Reminder that Legacy points **cannot be refunded**.

### 🏆 Legacy challenges
- All **66 challenges**, done and pending, with progress bars (levels, professions, honor, reputations, raids…).
- **Sorted by ease**: what you are closest to comes first.
- **Hide done** to see only what is left.
- **Click** a challenge to open it in the game's Legacy Challenges window.

### 📜 Trainer reminder
- When you **level up**, an animated banner in the middle of the screen, like the game's own: the **new spells and ranks** your trainer has for you, their icons and tooltips, and the **total cost**.
- Says whether **your gold is enough** or how much you are short.
- Ranks the trainer does not sell are named apart, with where they come from (**quest**, **drop**, **vendor**).
- Prices from Wowhead, replaced by the **real prices** (reputation discount included) the first time you open your trainer.
- `/zb trainer` shows the list any time; `/zb trainertest` previews the banner.

### Other
- Everything translated into the 11 languages.
